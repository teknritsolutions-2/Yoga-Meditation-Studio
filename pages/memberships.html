How the comparison is calculated

Expected visits = classes per week × validity days ÷ 7, rounded up, capped at the pack’s credit limit. Cost per attended class = plan price ÷ expected usable visits, rounded up. Unlimited plans use all expected visits. This compares one plan at a time; unused credits do not roll over in this calculation.

What’s included?

All plans cover regular Yoga, Meditation, and Breathwork sessions. Workshops and special events are excluded.

When does my class pack expire?

Single Session: 7 days. Five-Class Pack: 30 days. Ten-Class Pack: 60 days. Unlimited Monthly: 30 days. These are indicative plan terms.

Does my plan renew automatically?

No. Renewal is manual. Explore your membership and renewal options in the member dashboard.

Can I change or renew a plan?

Open the member dashboard to explore a renewal or plan-change request. No payment is collected without a connected checkout.
