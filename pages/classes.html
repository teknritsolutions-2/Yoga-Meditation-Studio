What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, water, and your own mat if you prefer. Confirm studio equipment with the team before your first visit.

Which class is right for a beginner?

Start with our Beginner Hatha or Restorative sessions. All Levels classes also offer a welcoming place to begin.

How does demo booking work?

Choose a session and open the demo member dashboard. One credit is held per eligible booking. Cancel any time before the sample session starts to restore your credit. This is a demonstration policy only.

Can I book without a membership?

Our illustrative Single Session plan gives you one credit. All plans and prices are examples until the studio confirms its offerings.
